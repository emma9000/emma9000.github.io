IoT Production Monitoring System

Industrial IoT platform collecting device data from production lines, monitoring status, and supporting manufacturing analytics.

Project Overview

Gathered requirements directly from production teams, then designed and built the backend of a shop-floor production data platform (production planning, process management, test records, device data collection) on three .NET 5 microservices with MySQL, Dapper, and Refit for inter-service calls. Integrated the company’s unified authentication platform for single sign-on, and an enterprise collaboration platform for notifications and approval workflows, replacing the manual tracking of maintenance, scrap and production progress. Scheduled 36 Hangfire jobs syncing ERP, SMT machine and test data into statistics tables for output, yield, equipment and plan dashboards. Developed a Quartz.NET Windows service that cleans and uploads SMT line inspection results to the cloud every 3 minutes.

Contributed to a VSTO Excel add-in for production planners that schedules daily, weekly and monthly plans through the platform's REST APIs, cutting a full planning cycle from about 3 to 5 days to within a day.

Also contributed to production test tooling: a C# desktop application that runs Lua test plans over Serial, UDP, and TCP against ZigBee and network devices, parsing the serial protocols of ZigBee modules from NXP and another vendor, with results reported to the platform for quality management.

About Me Software engineer with a surveying & GIS background, based in Auckland, NZ

Get to know me!

I'm Emma Xu, a software engineer based in Auckland with seven years of .NET/C# backend experience, including three years leading cross-functional teams. I'm currently completing a Master of Software Engineering in New Zealand.

Before software, I trained and worked in surveying and GIS — a BEng and MSc in Surveying & Mapping Engineering, then two years building desktop GIS tooling at SuperMap. That combination is unusual: most backend engineers haven't spent years thinking in coordinate systems and spatial data models, and it still shapes how I approach systems work today.

More recently I've moved into full-stack and Web3 — building and operating a real-time translation platform solo, and shipping nine DeFi modules end-to-end during an internship at a blockchain education startup.

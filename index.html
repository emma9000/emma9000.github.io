About Me Full-stack software engineer with seven years of .NET background, based in Auckland, NZ

Get to know me!

I'm Emma Xu, a software engineer based in Auckland with seven years of .NET/C# backend experience, including three years leading cross-functional teams. I've completed a Master of Software Engineering in New Zealand.

I work primarily in .NET, and have grown into full-stack and Web3 — building and operating a real-time translation platform for community organisations (manytongues.live), a Web3 job-hunting platform (jobdao.org), and DeFi modules during an industry blockchain internship.

My MSc in Surveying & Mapping Engineering — followed by a BEng in the same field — is where GIS comes from; I then spent two years as a software developer building desktop GIS tooling at KQ GEO Technologies, before moving into web development with front-end/back-end separated architectures. That background still shapes how I think about systems today.
